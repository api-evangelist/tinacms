The editorial workflow in TinaCMS is one of its most powerful features, enabling structured content updates through branches and pull requests. While feedback on the feature has been largely positive, one recurring issue stood out: users were unintentionally losing their pull requests due to gaps in the user experience. This update focuses on making the workflow more resilient, more intuitive, and easier to follow from start to finish.
