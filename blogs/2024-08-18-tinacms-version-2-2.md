We are excited to announce the release of tinacms@2.2.0 and @tinacms/cli@1.6.0! 🚀 Finding the right image or video is now easier than ever with infinite scroll in the Media Manager, and you can quickly locate files with the new file path display in collection items. Plus, the Markdown Editor is way better, the toolbar is improved, and you can enhance your content creation with the upgraded Plate version 36.
